# References

Use these sources as research starting points. Verify the relevant edition, version, and passage before attributing a specific claim.

## Editing and visual language

- [Walter Murch — *In the Blink of an Eye*, 2nd edition](https://www.silmanjamespress.com/shop/filmmaking-directing/in-the-blink-of-an-eye2nd-edition/): editing criteria and continuity. Do not treat the Rule of Six weights as measured effectiveness.
- [Christopher Bowen — *Grammar of the Edit*, 5th edition](https://www.routledge.com/Grammar-of-the-Edit/Bowen/p/book/9781032190150): a study reference for editing grammar. Verify any specific taxonomy against the book.
- Edward Dmytryk — *On Film Editing*: classic discussion of cut motivation, timing, and continuity.
- Sergei Eisenstein — *Film Form* and *The Film Sense*: montage and audiovisual relationships.
- Bruce Block — *The Visual Story*: visual structure, contrast, space, color, movement, and rhythm.
- Richard Williams — *The Animator's Survival Kit*: timing, spacing, anticipation, and overlap.
- Frank Thomas and Ollie Johnston — *The Illusion of Life*: animation principles. Applying those principles to motion graphics is an adaptation by this project.

## Contemporary examples and analysis

- [This Guy Edits](https://www.youtube.com/@ThisGuyEdits): editing, dramatic pacing, and narrative.
- [The Editing Podcast](https://www.youtube.com/@TheEditingPodcast): long-form discussions of professional editing.
- [Thomas Flight](https://www.youtube.com/@ThomasFlight): audiovisual grammar and film analysis.
- [StudioBinder](https://www.youtube.com/@StudioBinder): camera, framing, and editing demonstrations.
- [Art of the Title](https://www.artofthetitle.com/): title design, kinetic typography, and art direction.
- [Hayden Hillier-Smith](https://www.youtube.com/@HillierSmith) and [Finzar](https://www.youtube.com/@Finzar): creator editing and visual comedy.
- [Johnny Harris](https://www.youtube.com/@johnnyharris): editorial composition, maps, and explanatory visuals.
- [Daniel Schiffer](https://www.youtube.com/@DanielSchiffer) and [Sam Kolder](https://www.youtube.com/@kold): B-roll, speed ramps, and camera movement.
- [The Rough Cut](https://theroughcutpod.com/) and [Mixing Light](https://mixinglight.com/): interviews and advanced finishing and grading material.

## Technical documentation

- [ITU-R BS.1770](https://www.itu.int/rec/R-REC-BS.1770): algorithms for integrated loudness and true-peak measurement. Confirm the applicable revision.
- [EBU R128](https://tech.ebu.ch/loudness): loudness normalization and loudness-range guidance.
- [FFmpeg documentation](https://ffmpeg.org/documentation.html): filters including `sidechaincompress`, `loudnorm`, `xfade`, and `ebur128`.
- [Aegisub ASS override tags](https://aegisub.org/docs/latest/ass_tags/): syntax and timing units for animated captions.
- [W3C WCAG 2.2 — Three Flashes or Below Threshold](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/three-flashes-or-below-threshold.html): a useful accessibility boundary for video flash and glitch effects.
- [FFprobe documentation](https://ffmpeg.org/ffprobe.html): container, metadata, and stream inspection.
- [Adobe Premiere transitions](https://helpx.adobe.com/premiere/desktop/add-video-effects/apply-video-transitions/transitions-overview.html): transition concepts and native effects.
- [YouTube automatic captions](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/6373554): limitations and review of automatic transcripts.

## Research notes

Books and creators listed here form a research map rather than proof of every technique in the skill. Before attributing a formula, number, or effect to an author, consult the specific work and record the relevant passage. Treat timing examples as creative hypotheses and validate them against the brief and the result.
